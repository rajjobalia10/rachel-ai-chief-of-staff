# Rachel brand identity

Rachel's identity is built around **calm command**: a trusted chief of staff who quietly keeps the day moving, asks before acting, and lives where the user already communicates.

## Core idea

- Brand promise: **Your day, already handled.**
- Product descriptor: **Your AI chief of staff in iMessage.**
- Personality: composed, perceptive, discreet, proactive, and human.
- Voice: short, specific, reassuring, and never theatrical.

## Identity system

### Mark and wordmark

- Primary lockup: the Rachel star mark followed by the `Rachel` wordmark in Inter.
- Product/avatar mark: the star alone inside Rachel's iMessage contact avatar and other high-signal identity moments.
- The star is a signature, not a pattern. Use it in the header, footer, browser icon, product identity, and occasional approval or connection moments; do not scatter it decoratively.
- Minimum digital size: 16 px for the star alone and 72 px for the complete lockup.
- Clear space around the mark: at least half the rendered mark width.

### Color

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rachel blue | `#0086FF` | Product action, status, and the brand mark |
| Ink | `#000000` | Primary typography and conversion surfaces |
| Paper | `#F7F7F7` | Page canvas and quiet neutral space |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Product cards and elevated surfaces |
| Muted | `#7A7A7A` | Secondary information outside the hero |
| Hero supporting text | `#666666` | Accessible supporting copy over the coastal hero |

Do not introduce another primary accent. Warm coastal light belongs to photography; Rachel blue remains the digital product signal.

### Typography

- Display: Google Sans Medium, weight 500.
- Body and UI: Inter, weights 400–700.
- Mobile navigation: Inter Display.
- Display copy should be compact, balanced, and sentence case. Body copy should sound like a precise person, not a software dashboard.

### Imagery

- Hero imagery: warm California coastal daylight, generous negative space, and a centered iPhone showing a real Rachel/iMessage exchange.
- Product imagery: bright, legible, restrained interface renders with Rachel blue as the connective signal.
- Photography: candid, quiet, premium, and naturally lit. Avoid generic futuristic AI tropes, neon grids, robots, or abstract brains.
- Every product visual must reinforce one of three ideas: Rachel remembers, Rachel coordinates, or Rachel asks before acting.

### Motion

- Motion communicates readiness and follow-through: one-time spring entrances, brief hover lifts, and direct state changes.
- The phone may settle into place once; it must not float perpetually.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere.

## Application rules

- Header and footer use the same lockup, spacing, and mark.
- Browser favicon and social preview use Rachel's mark and hero art direction.
- iMessage visuals identify Rachel with the star mark wherever the asset supports it; avoid generic letter avatars in new work.
- Buttons use the existing black conversion treatment; Rachel blue indicates product state, connection, and confirmation.
- Poke and Pally inform product positioning and copy only. Slate remains the interface and motion source of truth. The supplied Rachel hero governs the first-screen format. Shantanu Kumar's Quick Brown Fox project informs the rigor of applying one coherent identity, not the visual style itself.
