07 / Guardrails

Consistency is the premium signal.

The strongest brand move is disciplined repetition: one mark, one lockup, one accent, one voice, and a clear boundary between atmosphere and proof.

Use

  • The tapered eight-ray mark, with its subtle lower-right conversational gesture, at true Rachel identity moments.
  • Google Sans for calm authority and Inter for precision.
  • Black conversion surfaces and blue product-state signals.
  • Warm editorial images for narrative; clean UI for proof.
  • Short, specific, approval-first language.

Avoid

  • New fonts, alternate marks, or decorative star patterns.
  • Additional primary colors or a warm recolor of product UI.
  • Generic AI imagery, stock photography, stock-style lifestyle scenes, generated people, and unsourced portraits.
  • Replacing user, tool, permission, or action icons with the mark.
  • Changing Slate-derived grids, spacing, shadows, or motion.

The system at the growth edge.

Community workflows, product principles or verified evidence, and the final conversion moment should feel like natural extensions of the same identity—not separate campaign ideas.

Application 01

Community proof

Lead with one real outcome in Google Sans, followed by a compact Inter row for the verified name, role/company, and optional permissioned logo.

Never invent names, companies, affiliations, quotes, outcomes, or claims. No stock portraits, generated people, or placeholder logos.

Application 02

Principles + metrics

Use large principle-led typography when operating data is unavailable. For metrics, lead with one verified Google Sans value and place its Inter label, timeframe, and source directly beside or beneath it.

Never invent customer names, activity, values, comparisons, or claims. No simulated counters, ornamental dashboards, or invented charts.

Application 03

Product-led close

End on a real Rachel workflow or iMessage outcome, then repeat one primary Text Rachel action.

The product is the proof. Preserve the black conversion surface and Rachel-blue state signal.

Identity-process context only: Shantanu Kumar's Quick Brown Fox work informs the rigor of building and applying a complete identity system. Rachel does not borrow its fox, coffee palette, typography, packaging, layouts, or visual style.
